How booking works

  1. Send your request

    Fill in the form below with your date, place and number of guests.

  2. Get a quote

    We reply with a menu suggestion and a price, usually the same day.

  3. Confirm and pay a deposit

    Your date is reserved once the deposit is agreed.

  4. We cook and deliver

    Fresh food arrives at your venue on time.

Book or request a contract

Your request is sent to us on WhatsApp or email, whichever you choose. We reply to confirm availability.

Questions people ask

How early should I book?

For events, book as early as you can. For urgent orders, call us any time; we are open 24 hours.

Do you do long-term contracts?

Yes. Choose "Contract catering" in the form and describe how many meals you need and how often.

Do you deliver?

We deliver within Lagos. Delivery cost depends on distance and is included in your quote.

Can you cook for special diets?

Tell us about allergies and dietary needs in the details box and we will plan the menu around them.
